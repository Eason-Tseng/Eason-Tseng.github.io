Support and FAQ

How do I move my data to a new phone?
AssetPilot has no cloud account, so you move data with an export file. On the old phone, go to Settings → Export all data and save the file to the Files app, a cloud drive, or email it to yourself. On the new phone, go to Settings → Import from export file and pick that file. Importing replaces all data on the new phone (an automatic export is made first). Afterwards, stop using the app on the old phone so the two copies don't drift apart.
How often should I export?
Your data lives only on this device and can't be recovered if the phone is lost or broken. When it has been more than 30 days since your last export and your data has changed, Overview reminds you to export.
Where do prices come from, and how often are they updated?
Taiwan stocks: daily closing prices from TWSE and TPEx. Crypto: CoinMarketCap, every 15 minutes. Exchange rates: derived daily from the Central Bank of Taiwan's interbank closing rate and ECB reference rates. See Settings → Data sources in the app.
It says prices can't be updated right now
You may be offline, the server may be temporarily unavailable, or the genuine-app check failed for now. The app keeps showing the last prices it received (with their data time). Pull down on Overview to try again later, or enter prices manually.
It says this device can't update prices automatically
Prices are provided only to genuine copies installed from the App Store or Google Play. Jailbroken or rooted devices and Android devices without Google Play services can't pass the check. All features still work; enter prices manually.
I can't unlock App Lock
App Lock uses your device's Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or passcode. Your device passcode always works.
