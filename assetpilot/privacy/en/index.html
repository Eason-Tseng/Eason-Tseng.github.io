AssetPilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

AssetPilot (listed as "AssetPilot: Allocation") is an app for recording and reviewing your personal asset allocation. This policy explains how the app and our server handle data.

In short: your asset data stays on your device. AssetPilot has no accounts or sign-in, does not track you, and shows no ads. Our server only provides market data (stock prices, crypto prices and exchange rates) and keeps no user data.

Developer and contact

Developer: ESN Labs (Eason-Tseng)

Privacy contact: g9008933@gmail.com

1. Data stored on your device

The accounts, holdings, quantities, prices, asset packs, charts, allocation strategies and daily snapshots you enter are stored only in a database on your device. They are never uploaded to our server or any cloud. Deleting the app deletes this data (export a backup first).

2. Data exchanged with our server

The server only provides market data. The app never sends your quantities, amounts, names or accounts.

WhenWhat the app sendsWhat the server keeps
Downloading prices and the instrument listAn access token that only proves "this is a genuine copy of the app"; it contains no user dataNothing. Rate-limit counters keep only a one-way hash of the token ID and are deleted within 2 days
Prices for less common cryptocurrencies (outside the top 500)The CoinMarketCap IDs of those coins you hold (no quantities or amounts)Only the coin ID and when it was last requested, not linked to any device or token; deleted after 30 days without requests
Genuine-app check (about once a week)iPhone: Apple App Attest data; Android: a Google Play Integrity tokenNothing. Used only to confirm the app was installed from the official store

IP address: any network connection reveals your IP address to the server. We use only a one-way hash of it for rate limiting (abuse prevention) and delete it within 2 days. Our hosting provider (Supabase) may keep standard access logs for a short period under its own policy.

Third parties for the genuine-app check: on Android, Google processes device and app integrity information for Play Integrity; on iPhone, Apple processes App Attest. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

3. Crash reports (optional, off by default)

You can turn on anonymous crash reports in Settings to help us fix problems. They are off by default, and nothing is sent while they are off. When on, a crash sends the following through Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): where the crash happened (stack trace), app version, device model and OS version. Amounts, names, accounts, codes, user names in file paths and anything typed on screen are removed before sending; IP addresses are not stored; reports are not linked to your identity or used for tracking. You can turn them off at any time.

4. Export files

"Export all data" creates a file with all your data in the app. You choose where to keep it (for example the Files app, a cloud drive, or an email to yourself) and you are responsible for it. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: to download market data and for the genuine-app check.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint (optional): used only if you turn on App Lock. The system performs the check; the app never receives biometric data.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notification permissions.

6. No accounts, tracking or ads

AssetPilot has no accounts or sign-in, uses no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not sell or share your data.

7. Not investment advice

AssetPilot only records and displays your assets. It does not transfer money, place orders, or provide investment advice.

8. Children

The app is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Deleting data and privacy requests

Your data lives only on your device: delete items in the app or delete the app. Our server holds nothing that can be linked to you, so there is no account data to request deletion of. For any privacy question, email the address above.

10. Changes

If the way we handle data changes significantly, we will update this page and its effective date.
